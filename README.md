## 🎯 Project Overview & Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze the sales and financial health of the Sanchhar e-commerce platform. By consolidating transactional data, this dashboard helps business stakeholders identify top-performing regions, monthly profit fluctuations, dominant payment channels, and product demand.

---

## 📈 Key Metrics & KPIs

- **Total Sales Amount:** 438K
- **Total Profit:** 37K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 6K units
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 121K

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Profit Drivers by Region:** Indore and Mumbai generated the highest profit, leading over Pune and Mathura.
- **Seasonality & Trends:** High profitability was sustained through Q1 (Jan–Mar) and late Q3/Q4 (Aug, Oct, Nov). July and December observed operating dips into negative margins.
- **Product Demand vs. Profit:**
  - **T-shirts (61%)** dominate order volume, followed by Trousers (27%) and Tables (12%).
  - **Printers** and **Bookcases** deliver the highest absolute profit despite lower sales volume compared to apparel.
- **Customer Payment Preference:** Cash on Delivery (COD) represents the largest share at **37%**, followed by UPI at **22%**, EMI at **17%**, and Cards (Debit/Credit) at **24%**.
- **Top Spenders:** Customers like Harivansh and Madhav represent the highest individual revenue contributors.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Tool:** Power BI Desktop / Power BI Service
- **Data Modeling:** Star Schema / Relationships
- **DAX Calculations:** Measures for Total Revenue, Total Profit, Average Order Value (AOV), and Quantity breakdown
- **Visuals Used:** KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Stacked Bar Charts, Waterfall/Clustered Column Charts, and Quarter/State Slicers
